Student housing is very expensive, and parking fees add a massive hidden cost. 
Reddit users warn that student apartments charge $100 to $400+ per month for parking, or sell semester permits for up to $738. 
Renters on Reddit say to lock your car and watch out for rent price hikes.

Source: Reddit

Content:

Review 1:
Parking is $150/mo for regular space and $175 for premium (next to elevator). 
I have a regular space but am close to the elevator still. Sign up for parking right away.

Review 2:
A resident parking permit for the campus garages (like the 7th Street or South Garage) costs about $400 per semester. 
Underground and reserved spots can run upwards of $600 per semester.

Review 3:
I have been getting countless emails and phone calls about renewing my lease (about 6 months into the 1 year lease). 
They tried really hard to pressure us into renewing by saying they will begin leasing out our unit if we don’t sign by the end of January, they will raise parking prices to $180/month for next year, and they open up our parking spot to the waitlist for next year.
