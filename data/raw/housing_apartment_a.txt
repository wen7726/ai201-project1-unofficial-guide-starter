Topic: student apartment review noisy maintenance 

Content:

Review 1:
Thin Walls: Many student flats are built fast with cheap wood. 
You can hear your upstairs neighbor walking or your roommates talking in the hall.

Review 2:
The Fix: Users suggest buying a good fan for white noise or wearing strong earplugs to sleep.

Review 3:
Quiet Hours: Most complexes have rules for quiet time after 10 PM. 
If people break the rules, you can file a formal complaint with the manager.
