Review 1:
if you count moving out for university, then I remember being really nervous. 
I made a checklist of stuff I needed (I scoured the internet for suggested checklists and adapted them) and then I checked it over several times. 
I had to go out and buy stuff I didn't have like my own set of pots and pans, cutlery, bedstuff, linens etc. That was quite exciting, I felt like I was being a more independent adult.

Review 2:
I'm starting to realize I have so much that I don't need. I hold on to miscellaneous crap that I dont need once I'm through with it. However, I don't have towels or cutlery. Luckily, I'm moving in wuth friends who have their own things, but I'd like to have my own as well.

Review 3:
moving out felt so good. To deal with all of these issues was worth it to feel like I was finally an adult and finally independent. 
I was able to do what I wanted when I wanted. I could eat what I wanted, sleep when I wanted, clean my bathroom when I wanted. 
My only advice is to make sure you do some serious research on places you want to move. Research the neighborhood, walk through the place, ask questions. 
Meet the neighbors before you sign, if you can. Make sure you've got money saved up for the unexpected. 
There are so many things you won't remember to get/think of, but need.
