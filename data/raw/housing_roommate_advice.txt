Topic: housing roomate advice 
Content:

Review 1:
Write down what you need to be comfortable. Do you need total quiet after 10 PM? Are you a neat freak?

Review 2:
Treat finding a roommate like finding a business partner. Talk about budgets, daily routines, and pet policies.

Review 3:
Write a document that covers chores, quiet hours, and guest policies. Treat it like a team playbook.

Review 4:
Create a shared system or app to split utilities, rent, and groceries. Figure out exactly who pays whom and when.
