Review 1:
Any of the new buildings built are all corporate housing and the management is awful. 
Often the calls to office get routed to an out of area call center where all they can do is take your name and say "someone will be back to you within 48-72 hours" this unfortunately includes maintenance. 
Being corporate housing, they could care less about break ins or fixing things in an appropriate manner, they really don't care. 
They hire poor property management people who clearly are annoyed to be working at their position.

Review 2:
Property managers want you to be an expert in 10 fields and pay you $20 an hour then act like they are doing you a favor.

Review 3:
Lots of complaints of terrible management, car break-ins in the garage, etc. I understand that generally the people leaving reviews are the ones who are unhappy with their situation. I also understand that any apartment near downtown will have some level of crime.
