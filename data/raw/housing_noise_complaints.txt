Review 1:
Keep making complaints and document it each time

Review 2:
Keep making written complaints to the landlord/ management office . Encourage your neighbors to do the same . 
Landlords don’t like complaints because it generates work for them , so the noisy tenants will be out in no time .

Review 3:
Post a notice in the common areas, and give your immediate neighbors the notice. With the managers number and e mail, police non Emergency numbers. 
Also, add to document or video the noise with the time of occurrence. This should get the ball rolling, and you won't be the only one complaining.
